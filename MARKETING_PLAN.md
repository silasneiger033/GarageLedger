# GarageLedger organic marketing launch

## Audience and positioning
DIY car owners who want to understand maintenance costs without a subscription. Lead with the free calculator, not the paid template.

## Week 1: trust and discoverability
- Verify public site URL, robots.txt and sitemap.xml, then submit sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Add a real workbook screenshot to Payhip (do not represent an illustration as a literal screenshot).
- Run a test purchase and confirm download delivery before promotion.
- Publish one transparent how-to post: “How to compare DIY vs garage repair costs” with an example and link to the free calculator.

## Weeks 2–4: organic traffic
- Post 2 short original demonstrations per week on Instagram or TikTok showing real workbook functions.
- Share one genuinely helpful maintenance-cost explanation in relevant communities, only where self-promotion is permitted.
- Track visits, clicks, conversion and refunds weekly; improve product description based on real objections.
- Create one SEO guide targeting “car maintenance spreadsheet template” and one targeting “DIY vs mechanic cost calculator”.

## Copy ideas
- “Stop guessing what your car really costs. Log services and fuel in one workbook.”
- “DIY or workshop? Compare parts and labor before you decide.”

## Ethics
No fake reviews, spam, fabricated results or claims of guaranteed savings. Never disclose customer data publicly.

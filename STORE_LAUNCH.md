# GarageLedger digital products

This folder contains the commercial product launch configuration.

## Launch checklist
1. Create a merchant account with a digital-download platform that supports Switzerland and your chosen payout method.
2. Upload the downloadable product files and verify that purchases deliver them automatically.
3. Set the public product URL in `store-config.js`.
4. Test checkout and delivery with a permitted test purchase before announcing the launch.
5. Publish accurate refund, contact, privacy and tax information.

No payment links or purchases are active by default. Do not commit payment secrets, customer records or API keys to this repository.

## Offers
- Free: GarageLedger browser-based vehicle tracker.
- Starter (planned CHF 9): downloadable vehicle maintenance spreadsheet.
- Bundle (planned CHF 19): spreadsheet plus ownership-cost and DIY comparison tools.

Prices are proposed, not currently charged.
